# Contribution rules

 - Do not send PRs whose code you left entirely to an AI
 - Using AI is allowed in itself, but you are expected to fully understand what it produced
 - PRs that do not follow these rules, or that the maintainers otherwise judge inappropriate, may be rejected
 - That said, AI-assisted translations are always welcome
 - When translating, please translate into English
 - To Japanese speakers: PRs in English are preferred if possible. Japanese PRs will not be rejected, but English is easier to manage.
